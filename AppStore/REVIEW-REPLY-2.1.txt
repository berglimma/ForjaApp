2) PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Forja is a productivity app that turns Pomodoro-style focus sessions into a forge/RPG fantasy experience. The user lights the forge, stays focused while ore heats, and earns in-app progress and collectibles.

Target audience: students and gamers who want focus tools with RPG aesthetics rather than a plain timer.

Problem solved: distraction and lack of motivation during study/work. Value: a clear focus ritual, daily limits for free users, progression (ore → crafts), weekly trail, and optional premium cosmetics/unlimited sessions via subscription.

Alternatively, reviewers can use the app immediately as Guest (anonymous Firebase session) without creating an account.

How to use main features:
1. Launch app → complete or skip onboarding (“Continuar no modo livre” / “Começar”).
2. Tab “Forja”: choose session duration → Start. Stay in the app during the countdown (leaving can fail the session after a short grace period).
3. On success, earn ore/bars → spend in Loja → Oficina.
4. Tabs: Forja (timer), Trilha (weekly path), Loja (shop), Guilda (guilds/challenges), Perfil (account, restore purchases, delete account).
5. Free users: up to 8 focus sessions per day. Mestre Ferreiro subscription removes the daily limit and unlocks premium cosmetics/stats/backup features.

Account deletion: Perfil → Excluir conta (deletes Firebase Auth user + Firestore user data; Apple ID purchases remain tied to the Apple ID and can be restored).
