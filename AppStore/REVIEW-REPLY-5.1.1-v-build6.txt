Hello App Review team,

We updated build 1.0 (6) to make account deletion easier to find and to work reliably on iPad and iPhone.

Account deletion path (in-app, permanent):
1. Sign in with the demo account (App Review Information username/password), or create a new account.
2. Open the Perfil tab (bottom bar).
3. Tap Conta e privacidade.
4. Tap Excluir conta permanentemente.
5. For email accounts, confirm with the account password, then confirm deletion.
6. The app deletes the Firebase Auth user and Firestore user document, clears local progress, and returns to a guest session.

This is a permanent delete (not deactivation). No website is required.

A new physical-device screen recording demonstrating the full flow is attached to this reply and will also be added to App Review Information notes for future submissions.
