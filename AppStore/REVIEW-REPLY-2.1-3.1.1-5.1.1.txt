Account deletion is available in the app:
• Sign in with the demo account (App Review Information username/password)
• Open the Perfil (Profile) tab
• Under “Conta e dados”, tap “Excluir conta e dados”
• Confirm in the dialog — this permanently deletes the Firebase Auth user and Firestore user document, then returns to anonymous session
