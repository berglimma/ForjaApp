2) PURPOSE / AUDIENCE
Forja is a productivity focus timer with forge/RPG fantasy. Users start a forge session, stay focused while ore heats, earn progress/collectibles. Audience: students/gamers who want motivation beyond a plain Pomodoro. Solves distraction; value = focus ritual + progression + optional premium.

3) HOW TO USE
Launch → onboarding or “modo livre”. Tab Forja: pick duration → Start (stay in app; leaving may fail session). Earn ore → Loja → Oficina. Tabs: Forja, Trilha, Loja, Guilda, Perfil. Free: 8 sessions/day. Account deletion: Perfil → Excluir conta.
